Texture derivatives use 512 square Lanczos resizing of original 1K sources; no chroma subsampling. Full source URLs, original and derived SHA-256 digests, physical tile dimensions and transformations are recorded in manifest.json. Normal maps are OpenGL (+Y) tangent-space; ARM maps store AO/Roughness/Metallic in R/G/B.
