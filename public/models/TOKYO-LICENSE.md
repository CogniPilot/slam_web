# Littlest Tokyo

Model and animation by Glen Fox, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution
as credited in the Three.js example. Author and source remain credited in the UI.

- Author: https://artstation.com/glenatron
- Artwork: https://artstation.com/artwork/1AGwX
- Model source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/models/gltf/LittlestTokyo.glb
- Attribution reference: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/webgl_animation_keyframes.html

Application adaptation: uniformly scaled to a 30 m footprint and repositioned;
original materials and animation tracks retained. Animation playback uses the
simulation timestamp. This artistic scene is not a surveyed urban environment.

Draco decoder files are copied from the pinned Three.js 0.180.0 package and
retain their upstream license notices.
