# Quadrotor model

This work is based on [Drone](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/drone-2588b8a0917d474e97886763c98a65af)
by [Cafitz3D](https://sketchfab.com/Cafitz3D), licensed under
[CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The original GLB is bundled unchanged from CogniPilot Rumoca's interactive
quadrotor example. SHA-256:
`6eb0d2b6ed92a83b89101f2acc33cd3328b3e166555c82bf757e4c358ae731f4`.
Display scale, centering and propeller pivots are applied at load time.
Motor order is front-right, rear-left, front-left, rear-right. Propeller angles
come from the Modelica plant's integrated motor speeds, in simulation time.
