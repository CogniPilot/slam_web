# Genuine Rumoca quadrotor plant

[LabQuadrotor.mo](../models/Vehicles/LabQuadrotor.mo) contains the pinned Rumoca interactive `QuadrotorSIL` plant, CMM `RigidBody6DOF` and its two quaternion functions, followed by the lab controller/interface wrapper. The browser receives the complete editable Modelica dependency closure without filesystem source roots. [Vendored sources, license and provenance](../models/upstream/quadrotor/NOTICE.md) record the exact revisions and hashes. Plant, motor, aerodynamic, quaternion and landing-contact equations are unchanged. The two quaternion functions retain their qualified package names; only their `within` headers become nested package declarations.

The wrapper preserves `forward`, `left`, `up`, `yaw`, both flight tours and held `commandTime`. Named arrays express the velocity controller, thrust/moment mixer and four motor commands. It converts the plant's NWU world to the lab's ENU world: east is minus west, north remains north, and up remains up. A fixed quaternion frame conversion and initial plant yaw preserve the lab's initial identity heading. The existing IMU and angular-rate interface stays FLU through the plant's `a_b` and `omega`; the plant's separate FRD sensor aliases remain unchanged.

Default mass is the example's 2 kg. The lab wrapper passes an ordinary `gravity=9.81` modifier to the unchanged plant, whose example default is 9.8, keeping the lab estimator and sensor equations coherent. Initial height remains 1.5 m through the plant clearance parameter. Motors now start at zero and follow the example's asymmetric, smoothly limited motor dynamics. These physical startup dynamics replace the retired plant's pre-spun simplified motors. Saved edited physics source is not rewritten.

`propellerAngles[4]` are unwrapped Modelica differential states initialized to zero. Their derivatives use actual `omega_m[4]`, with signs `{+,+,-,-}`: front-right and rear-left CCW, front-left and rear-right CW. Three.js receives the phases to pose the asset; it does not integrate motor motion.

The [actual-WASM probe](../tests/compiler-probes/modelica-upstream-quadrotor.test.ts) passes on the unchanged production compiler pin. It checks 90 complete frames, world/body frame relationships, finite coherent truth/IMU fields, normalized attitude, rotor direction and speed bounds, exact 90-frame reset/replay, and an edited initial-height source. The existing snapshot test also passes with actual mass 2→2.4 editing, all four phases and interface refusal. The whole-plant tour comparison retains its `2e-8` bound; test integration accuracy was tightened for the genuine faster motor lag. Exact results, source hashes, resources and earlier harness/accuracy failures are in the [verification record](../dev/modelica-upstream-quadrotor-verification.json).

Production build and hardware renderer/browser acceptance of this new plant remain pending root integration. The focused numeric sequence is airborne; landing equations are preserved exactly, but a separate landing-equilibrium numerical oracle is not claimed. This dynamics migration does not establish SLAM or the 10× throughput target.
